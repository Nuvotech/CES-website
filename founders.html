Our Founders

The people who started Congo Service Engineering — and the story of how CSE grew.

Co-founder & Managing Director

Jean-Pierre Mukendi Kabongo

Born in Lubumbashi, Jean-Pierre studied civil engineering at the Université de Lubumbashi before spending over a decade on construction and mining infrastructure projects across Haut-Katanga and Lualaba.

As Managing Director he leads CSE’s strategy, client partnerships and project delivery, with a focus on building local engineering talent.

  • M.Sc. Civil Engineering
  • Project delivery & client relations
Co-founder & Technical Director

Grâce Mbuyi Tshibangu

A graduate of the Université de Kinshasa, Grâce built her career in plant maintenance and electrical installations for industrial operators in Kinshasa and Kongo Central.

As Technical Director she oversees engineering standards, safety and quality across every CSE site.

  • B.Eng. Electromechanical Engineering
  • Industrial & electrical systems

Our history

How CSE grew

Key moments from founding to today.

  1. 2009

    CSE is founded in Lubumbashi

    Jean-Pierre Mukendi Kabongo and Grâce Mbuyi Tshibangu open a small office with a team of six, offering civil works and electrical installations to local businesses.

  2. 2012

    First major mining contract

    CSE delivers site infrastructure and power distribution for a copper concentrator near Kolwezi — the project that established its reputation in the Copperbelt.

  3. 2015

    Industrial & mechanical division

    A dedicated team for plant installation, mechanical commissioning and process upgrades is created to serve mining and manufacturing clients.

  4. 2018

    Kinshasa office opens

    Expansion to the capital brings commercial construction, infrastructure and public-sector projects in Kinshasa and Kongo Central.

  5. 2021

    Technical maintenance services

    Long-term maintenance contracts launch, keeping plants, substations and equipment running for clients across Haut-Katanga and Lualaba.

  6. Today

    A national engineering partner

    More than 250 engineers and technicians, over 120 completed projects, and a growing presence in North Kivu and beyond.
